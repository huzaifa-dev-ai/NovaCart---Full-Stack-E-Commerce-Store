Help & policies

Everything about how your order ships, how returns work, and how we handle your data. Can't find it here? Contact us.

Shipping information

Orders placed before 3pm ship the same business day; everything else is dispatched within 24 hours. You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse.

  • Free standard shipping on all orders of $50 or more.
  • Standard shipping under $50: flat $4.99, delivered in 2–4 business days.
  • Express shipping: $12.99, delivered in 1–2 business days.
  • We currently ship to addresses within the country; international shipping is on the roadmap.

Returns & refunds

Changed your mind? No problem. Every order is covered by a 30-day hassle-free returns policy — no forms to print, no interrogation.

  • Start a return within 30 days of delivery from your orders page, or just contact us.
  • Items should come back in their original packaging with all accessories.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3–5 business days of the item reaching us.
  • Faulty on arrival? We'll send a replacement and a prepaid return label immediately.

Privacy policy

We collect the minimum needed to run your order and nothing more. Plainly: your data is not our product.

  • We store your name, contact details and order history to fulfil purchases and provide support.
  • Your cart is saved locally in your browser; it never leaves your device until you place an order.
  • We never sell or rent personal data to third parties.
  • Payment details are processed by our payment provider and never touch our servers.
  • Email us any time to request a copy of your data or its deletion.
